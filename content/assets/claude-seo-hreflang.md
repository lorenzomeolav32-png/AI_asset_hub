## Overview

**Hreflang / International SEO Audit** runs `/seo hreflang <url>` in the
[Claude SEO](/claude-skills/claude-seo) suite, auditing and generating
hreflang tags and flagging machine-translation quality issues per the January
2025 Quality Rater Guidelines.
