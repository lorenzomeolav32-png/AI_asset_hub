## Overview

**Job Application Message** writes the short note that goes in the "Message" box of a
job form or portal — not a cover letter. It shows genuine interest and connects your
real experience to exactly what the role asks for.

## How it works

You paste the job posting and attach your CV (a photo works). The skill reads your
actual roles, skills and metrics and mirrors the posting's language — never inventing
experience.

1. **Read the posting** — figure out what the employer really wants.
2. **Read your CV** — extract real roles, tools, certs and numbers.
3. **Mirror the language** — match required skills with your words.
4. **Keep it short** — 3–6 sentences, ~40–90 words, professional but human.

## Examples

```txt
Interest in one line ("Excited to apply for the L2 Support role at Acme").
Two lines connecting your real tickets/tools to their requirements.
One closing line — availability / happy to share more.
```

Great for LinkedIn, company career pages and any form with a tiny message box.
