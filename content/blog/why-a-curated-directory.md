There are already directories with tens of thousands of AI assets. We built a
smaller one on purpose.

## The problem with big catalogs

When a directory crawls everything, you get volume and very little trust. You
still have to open each result, read it, and guess whether it works. Size stops
helping once you cannot tell the good entries from the broken ones.

## What we do instead

We keep the list short and check the things that waste your time when they are
wrong: the source, the license, and whether the install steps actually run. If
we have not tested something, we do not put a verified mark on it. The catalog
grows slower this way, and that is the point.

## Who this is for

Developers and builders who want a config they can copy and trust, not a search
result they have to audit. If you would rather scroll a hundred thousand entries
and filter them yourself, the big crawlers already do that well.

## Where this goes

We are starting with Claude Skills and MCP servers, then adding Copilot agents,
Cursor rules, and workflows. Next to the directory we write short guides on how
to use these tools, because knowing a skill exists is only half the job.
